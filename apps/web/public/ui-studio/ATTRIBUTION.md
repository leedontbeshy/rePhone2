# Storefront artwork

Studio product illustrations, banners and the authentication backdrop were generated
with OpenAI Image Generation and encoded as WebP for this application. They are
decorative marketing artwork, not evidence of an individual inventory unit's condition.

Apple-source model reference URLs for sample catalog photography are recorded in
`apps/api/prisma/rich-catalog-model-sources.ts` with their educational demo usage basis.
Apple and iPhone names remain the property of their respective owners.
