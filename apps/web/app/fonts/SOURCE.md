The application ships `inter-latin-vietnamese.woff2`, sourced from the Google Fonts
distribution of Inter 4.001 (`git-66647c0bb`). It contains Latin and Vietnamese glyphs.
The weight axis was limited to 400–900 with fontTools; glyph outlines, metrics and
hinting were preserved. The application exposes weights 400–700.
